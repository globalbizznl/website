Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16-Aug-2026

GlobalBizz Solutions ("we", "us"), registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK 94673543), respects your privacy. This page explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and what rights you have.

What we collect

When you submit our request/brochure form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Any message or details you choose to include

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising pixels on this site. Our photo gallery loads external content (Google Drive)

Why we collect it

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry — for example, sending you our brochure, answering a wholesale request, or discussing a custom order. We do not use it for marketing unless you separately opt in, and we never sell or rent your data to third parties.

Legal basis

We process your data based on your consent (given when you submit the form) and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries sent to us.

Who else sees it

Your form submission is stored via Google Forms/Google Workspace, which acts as our data processor under its own data processing terms. We don't share your details with any other third party.

Because Google's infrastructure operates globally, your data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, under Google's standard contractual clauses and safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to handle your request and follow up, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict or object to how we use it
  • Receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these, email us — see below. You can also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
